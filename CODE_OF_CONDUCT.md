# Community guidelines

Help keep the project useful and welcoming.

- Treat people with respect. Discuss the behavior of the software rather than attacking a person.
- Keep feedback relevant, clear and constructive.
- Do not post harassment, threats, personal information, credentials or private messages.
- Use the issue templates and search for existing reports before opening a duplicate.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close issues or restrict participation to protect the project and its community. Sensitive security concerns should use [private security reporting](SECURITY.md).
